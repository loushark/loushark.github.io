Bicycles

A trip to New York City inspired a series of bicycle paintings. I was aware of the abandoned bicycles throughout the city and I became intrigued by them.

I initially toyed with various ideas as the newly inspired work took shape. The work combined a range of styles and ideas, including postcard sized images and various styles of print, not casting aside my traditional form of painting.

Following this inspiration, I made a visit to Amsterdam, a city of bicycles, which only furthered my ambition to extend the bicycle series. I became interested in the linear form of the bicycle — purely a form to play with on the canvas, becoming more expressive and colourful as the series progressed.

The series has also been shown in many bicycle themed exhibitions in both the United Kingdom and the United States.
